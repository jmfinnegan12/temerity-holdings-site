# Magazine

Temerity Holdings Magazines are series of AI-curated research issues on topics central to the fund's work. Each issue is a combination of freely available information (articles, papers, journals) on the topic and Claude's synthesis of that information. Some issues will contain my personal notes on applications to the project or things I found interesting.

**What's in each issue:**
- Claude-authored synthesis sections
- Curated sources — the best freely available articles, papers, and reports on the topic
- A *My Notes* section where I add my notes
